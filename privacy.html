Word Link by Global Nova Technologies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Word Link: Puzzle Royale ("Word Link", "the app") is a word puzzle game made by Global Nova Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and the choices you have.

In short: Word Link does not require an account, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not send your personal information to us or to anyone else. Your game progress is stored on your own device.

1. Information stored on your device

To run the game, the app saves the following only on your phone:

  • Game progress: levels reached, stars, score, coins, hints, streak, achievements, tile styles you unlocked and recently played words (so puzzles don't repeat).
  • Player name and avatar, if you choose to enter a name. This is optional and never leaves your device.
  • Settings: sound, vibration and daily reminder preferences.

We do not have access to this information.

2. Device backup

Android may include the app's saved progress in your device's own backup to your Google account, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is created and managed by Google under your Google account settings and Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot see it. You can turn device backup off in your phone's settings.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a once-a-day reminder on your device to help you keep your streak. No information is sent to a server to do this. You can turn the reminder off in the app under Settings → Daily Reminder, or in your phone's notification settings.

4. Sharing your results

When you tap "Share Result", the app opens your phone's share menu with a short text summary of your result (for example, the level, stars and score). Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

5. Links

The app can open this website and your email app if you tap Privacy Policy, Terms of Use or Contact Support. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • We don't use advertising, advertising IDs or third-party analytics.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

7. Children

Word Link is a general-audience game. Because the app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Deleting your data

All game data is on your device. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Reset Progress in the app, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app. See Delete your data for details.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add features that handle information differently (for example, optional cloud saving of progress), we will update this policy and the effective date above before those features are released.
