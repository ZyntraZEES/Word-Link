Delete your data

Word Link: Puzzle Royale · Global Nova Technologies

Word Link has no accounts and keeps your game data only on your device, so you can delete all of it yourself, at any time, without contacting us.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Word Link and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Reset Progress, then Reset Everything.

This deletes your levels, stars, score, coins, hints, streak, achievements, unlocked tile styles, player name and settings.

Option 2: From your phone's settings

Go to Settings → Apps → Word Link → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Both remove all of the app's data from the device.

Device backups

If Android device backup is turned on, a copy of the app's data may exist in your Google account backup. You can manage or delete backups in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.

What is kept

Nothing. We don't hold any of your data on our servers.
